# Walker v. Bates

**Case:** Walker v. Bates, docket 93-2107

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-04-29; 23 F.3d 652 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Reversed dismissal of the claim against Bates and remanded.

**Historical panel:** Wilfred Feinberg, Roger J. Miner, J. Daniel Mahoney.

**Opinion author and joins:** Roger J. Miner wrote the opinion. Joined by Feinberg.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The court rejected a categorical rule that administrative reversal cures unconstitutional disciplinary punishment already served. It distinguished the administrative-review line of cases and allowed the § 1983 claim to proceed, without adjudicating all elements of liability or damages.

**Separate opinions:** Mahoney dissented, defending the administrative-cure framework and disagreeing that the later immunity discussion justified the majority’s treatment of circuit precedent.

**Remedy and mandate:** Further proceedings on the claim against Bates; the decision did not award a specified amount of damages or automatically restore every previously dismissed defendant.
