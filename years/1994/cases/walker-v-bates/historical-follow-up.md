## Dependence on the historical judgment

- **Certiorari petition (No. 94-158):** Sought by the hearing officer, the party aggrieved by reversal of the Rule 12(b)(6) dismissal. It addressed the holding that administrative reversal does not cure an unconstitutional disciplinary punishment already served. It depends on a reversal. An affirmance would leave Walker as the aggrieved party, and Bates would not petition. The Mahoney companion depends on the same principle.
- **Remand ruling and 1997 appeal:** Depend on reversal with remand and on the majority leaving qualified immunity undecided. The remand ruling relied on Sandin and on immunity, so the path survives only if the case reached remand with both questions open.
