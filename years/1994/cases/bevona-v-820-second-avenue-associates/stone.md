**Component: Arbitrability and reconsideration.**  
Propose affirmance under § 1291. Apply *AT&T Technologies* and the contract’s actual allocation rather than enlarging arbitration because the union’s objective is understandable. The newly identified employees do not establish coverage where their undisputed work differs materially from the contractor work.

**Holding and limits:** The agreement does not compel this grievance on the record presented. Reserve disputes in which covered employees did perform the same work or other language brings contractor employees within the agreement. No merits ruling on wrongful discharge is necessary. Stone’s support for collective labor activity operates through enacted and contractual rights, not an invented agreement.
