# Bevona v. 820 Second Avenue Associates

**Event:** June 16, 1994. **Panel:** McLaughlin, Jacobs, Weinstein. **Writing:** Per curiam; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** Affirmed vacatur of the award and denial of reconsideration. The contractor employees were outside the relevant contractual coverage, and the superintendent and handyman evidence did not alter that conclusion. **Mandate issuance:** Not verified. **Premise differences:** Jacobs’s proposed replacement only. ([Justia Law](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/27/37/541697/))
