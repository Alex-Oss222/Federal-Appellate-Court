# Jeffries v. Harleston

**Status:** Version 1; user-approved. **Baseline:** April 18, 1994 packet. **Approval reference:** User instruction of 2026-10-12 approving all eighteen Cycle 001 briefs as written ("all ... approved - change"), subject to each brief’s stated conditions. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Liability and immunity.**  
Within § 1291, propose affirmance of the supported speech-liability and immunity rulings. The legal relationship is application of the then-binding *Pickering*, *Connick*, and *Piesco* framework, not creation of an immunity-free speech rule. The jury’s finding of no actual impairment matters because the controlling circuit doctrine makes it matter. The university’s objection to the speech’s content cannot replace the operational showing required by that doctrine. Individual causation must remain defendant-specific. ([Law Resource](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/021/21.F3d.1238.93-7876.953.html))

**Component: Relief.**  
Propose preserving the two-year reinstatement remedy while vacating punitive damages for a new trial confined to that issue. The reasonable-expectation finding cannot simply be ignored when assessing punitive culpability. The remedy should restore the lost appointment period without creating future tenure as chair. This proposal applies Stone’s commitment to protected expression together with his separate requirements for causation, immunity, and remedy.
