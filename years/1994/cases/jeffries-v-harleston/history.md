# Jeffries v. Harleston

**Event:** April 18, 1994. **Panel:** Van Graafeiland, McLaughlin, Burns. **Author:** McLaughlin; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** The court sustained the liability and qualified-immunity rulings and the two-year reinstatement remedy, but vacated punitive damages and remanded for a new trial on that component. Its analysis applied the circuit’s then-existing public-employee speech doctrine and addressed the tension among the special findings. **Mandate date:** Not independently verified. **Premise differences:** McLaughlin’s proposed replacement; no later Supreme Court event is included in this comparator.
