# Young (Ramadan) v. Selsky

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Feinberg wrote the opinion, joined by Judge Winter, reversing the absolute-immunity ruling and remanding. Judge Van Graafeiland dissented, placing greater weight on Selsky's appellate function and the practical importance of immunity from repeated inmate litigation. *Young*, 41 F.3d at 53-61.

Stone's proposed disposition matches the historical result but makes no assumption that Feinberg would join every formulation of his reasoning. Neither the historical judgment nor Stone's proposal establishes liability, eliminates qualified immunity, or supplies a new simulation holding until approved.
