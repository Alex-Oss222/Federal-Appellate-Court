# Young (Ramadan) v. Selsky

**Case:** Young (Ramadan) v. Selsky, docket 93-2567

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-11-21; 41 F.3d 47 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Reversed the absolute-immunity dismissal and remanded.

**Historical panel:** Wilfred Feinberg, Ellsworth A. Van Graafeiland, Ralph K. Winter, Jr..

**Opinion author and joins:** Wilfred Feinberg wrote the opinion. Joined by Winter.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The court applied functional immunity analysis and held that Selsky’s administrative appellate position did not establish the judicial independence and safeguards required for absolute immunity. The decision left qualified immunity, the merits of the disciplinary claims, personal responsibility, and relief for further proceedings.

**Separate opinions:** Van Graafeiland dissented, giving greater weight to the adjudicative appellate function and practical protection against repeated prisoner litigation.

**Remedy and mandate:** Further proceedings on the claims against Selsky, without an automatic damages award, expungement, or revival of a different action.
