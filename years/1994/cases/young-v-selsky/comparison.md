## Historical comparison extract

Historically, on 1994-11-21 a Second Circuit panel of Judges Feinberg, Van Graafeiland and Winter reversed the absolute-immunity dismissal of the damages claims against Donald Selsky and remanded. Judge Feinberg wrote the opinion, joined by Judge Winter; Judge Van Graafeiland dissented, weighting the adjudicative appellate function and protection against repeated prisoner suits. The court applied functional immunity analysis and held that Selsky's administrative appellate position did not establish the judicial independence and safeguards absolute immunity requires, leaving qualified immunity, the merits, personal responsibility and relief to further proceedings, with no automatic damages award or expungement. The simulated judgment matches on disposition, 2 to 1 split, author, dissenter, remedy and functional-immunity ground; the only difference is that Judge Stone joins Feinberg's opinion in Winter's place. The judgment is unchanged, and no change of reasoning is found. Source: [Young v. Selsky, 41 F.3d 47 (2d Cir. 1994)](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/41/47/564093/), at 53-61.
