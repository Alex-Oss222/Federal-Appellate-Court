# Pagnucco v. Pan American World Airways, Inc.

**Case:** Pagnucco v. Pan American World Airways, Inc., docket 92-9251, 92-9253, 92-9255

**Historical event and date:** Amended panel disposition on reconsideration on 1994-09-12; 37 F.3d 804 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Affirmed the liability verdicts; affirmed loss-of-society awards to the Bainbridge and Porter families; vacated the Pagnucco family’s loss-of-society award; vacated parental-care awards in all three cases; remanded the identified damages issues.

**Historical panel:** Ellsworth A. Van Graafeiland, Richard J. Cardamone, Frank X. Altimari.

**Opinion author and joins:** Richard J. Cardamone wrote the opinion. Joined by Altimari.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The amended majority rejected the asserted liability-trial errors, including the regulatory-waiver and evidentiary challenges, and sustained willful-misconduct liability and causation. On damages it held that the Warsaw Convention permitted loss-of-society recovery by spouses and dependents. Bainbridge and Porter involved spouses and dependent children, so their society awards stood. Some Pagnucco children were adults whose dependency had not been established, requiring further proceedings on that award. Parental care was a compensable pecuniary category, but all three juries lacked the required minority limitation absent specific proof that guidance had pecuniary value beyond majority. The financial-contribution and service awards were not challenged. Source: 37 F.3d at 828-30, Part IV and Conclusion.

**Separate opinions:** Van Graafeiland dissented on liability, criticizing exclusion of defense evidence, the treatment of regulatory communications and alternative defenses, and the conduct of the trial. He agreed with the need for the identified damages correction.

**Remedy and mandate:** Limited remand for the Pagnucco loss-of-society award and parental-care awards in all three cases; liability and the unaffected damages components remained in place. The separate mandate’s issuance date and any stay or recall status are not established by an independently supplied docket and remain unknown.
