# Pagnucco v. Pan American World Airways, Inc.

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Cardamone wrote the amended majority opinion, joined by Judge Altimari. The court affirmed liability and much of the damages disposition, but vacated specified loss-of-society and parental-care components for further proceedings. Judge Van Graafeiland dissented from the liability affirmance and agreed with the damages remand. *Pagnucco*, 37 F.3d at 804-48.

Stone's proposal differs from the liability judgment and shares the dissent's concern about excluding mental-state evidence. It does not adopt every criticism of the trial or presume that every excluded item was admissible and preserved. The damages analysis operates within the law available on September 12, 1994; subsequent Supreme Court treatment of Warsaw damages is not imported.
