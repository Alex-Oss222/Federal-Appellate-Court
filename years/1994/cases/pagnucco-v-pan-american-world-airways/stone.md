# Pagnucco v. Pan American World Airways, Inc.

**Status:** Version 1; proposed, not user-approved. **Approval reference:** None. The source brief states that every Stone position, alternative, and negotiating limit is a proposal pending approval.

## Stone's proposed judicial position

Stone should propose a new trial on willful-misconduct liability because the preserved exclusion of Cozzi's mental-state evidence rested on an improper equivalence between legal authorization and the defendant's actual understanding. His opinion should expressly reject a defense of binding oral waiver. Pan Am remained subject to the written security requirements. The question is whether the people directing its operations possessed the culpable state of mind required to remove the Convention's liability limit.

A jury could disbelieve Cozzi, conclude that reliance was contrived, or find that even an honestly understood communication did not overcome knowledge of probable injury. Those are reasons to test the evidence, not to declare it legally irrelevant. Evidence that a responsible official instructed personnel to consult the regulator, received a report of approval, and proceeded on that understanding has some tendency to distinguish a consciously reckless operational choice from a mistaken judgment. Rule 105 permits the court to tell the jury that no legally effective waiver occurred and that the evidence bears only on the appropriate mental-state question. Rule 403 remains available for a particularized objection; it cannot sustain an exclusion whose stated foundation denied the distinction altogether.

The error's consequence must be considered against plaintiffs' substantial warning evidence. Stone should not minimize prior bomb incidents, the shortcomings of x-ray detection, or the alleged failure to respond adequately to specific warnings. Yet the issue is not whether that evidence would permit another jury to find willful misconduct. It is whether exclusion of the defense's account of its own decisionmaking could materially have affected this jury's resolution of the central disputed mental element. A trial built around management's knowledge and disregard is not fairly assessed by counting the remaining adverse incidents. The Cozzi exclusion, reinforced by the improper restriction on alternative regulatory defenses, warrants retrial under the civil substantial-rights standard.

The mandate should not require admission of every Sonesen statement, every proposed expert, or every alternative bombing scenario. Those matters retain their independent preservation, foundation, hearsay, and cumulative-evidence requirements. Nor should it direct judgment for Pan Am on causation. The circumstantial chain was sufficient to be evaluated by a jury; a new trial addresses a defective process of deciding liability, not an appellate determination of how the bomb actually traveled.

Stone should resolve the recurring damages issues necessary to guide further proceedings. Under the governing circuit framework, the spouse and dependent-child society claims are legally available; Pagnucco's adult children's dependency cannot be assumed. Parental-care instructions require the minority limitation and a separate evidentiary basis for identifiable post-majority pecuniary contributions. Any new damages proceedings must avoid double counting. The district court should determine which unchallenged and genuinely separable components can remain fixed, subject to the new liability determination, rather than automatically reopening every dollar or treating all existing awards as collectible despite vacated liability. Master Jurisprudence §§ 6, 8-9, 24-25; Evidence, Proof, and the Record; Statutory Interpretation; Remedies.

## Proposed negotiating limits

**Preferred and broadest defensible holding:** a preserved and prejudicial mental-state exclusion requires a new liability trial; actual legal authority and evidence relevant to culpable understanding must be separately analyzed. The opinion should also state the correct society and parental-care rules governing the remand.

**Acceptable narrower proposal:** a new trial based specifically on the Cozzi proffer and the restriction on alternative defenses, without deciding the disputed adequacy of other offers or announcing a general rule admitting regulatory communications. A remand confined to particular damages categories is acceptable only if Stone independently concludes that the liability error was harmless; that conclusion is not approved merely as a bargaining alternative.

**Non-join limits:** an effective oral waiver contrary to the governing regulatory procedure; automatic willful misconduct from any violation; exclusion of all state-of-mind evidence because it cannot establish authorization; appellate adoption of an unproved bomb route; unrestricted adult-child society awards; or an assumption that the Convention's loss of a limitation authorizes punitive damages.
