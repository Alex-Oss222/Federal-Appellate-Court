## Historical comparison

The historical event is the amended panel disposition on reconsideration, 1994-09-12, 37 F.3d 804. The panel (Van Graafeiland, Cardamone, Altimari) affirmed the willful-misconduct liability verdicts and rejected the regulatory-waiver and other evidentiary challenges. On damages it held that loss-of-society recovery is available to spouses and dependents, left the Bainbridge and Porter society awards in place, vacated the Pagnucco society award because adult-child dependency was not established, and vacated the parental-care awards in all three cases for want of a minority limitation absent specific proof of pecuniary value beyond majority. Financial-contribution and service awards were unchallenged and stand. Cardamone wrote, joined by Altimari. Van Graafeiland dissented on liability (exclusion of defense evidence, regulatory communications, alternative defenses, conduct of the trial) and agreed with the damages correction. The remedy was a limited remand.

The premises survive almost entirely: same record, same law at the cutoff, same rehearing posture, same two retained judges. The only declared change is the third seat, which alters neither the record, the authorities, nor the questions the two retained judges face. History is therefore strong evidence for both, though history.md states results and general grounds, not whether the majority found the Cozzi and British-evidence rulings non-erroneous or harmless.
