## Record and issues

APL carried bagged rice to Mozambique under a charter assigning responsibility for stevedores to APL. Cargo records reported shortages and damage. Webster pursued recovery for subrogated cargo insurers. The charter required three arbitrators who were “commercial men.” The chosen chair, Bartholomew Hennessey, had substantial shipping, claims, and insurance experience as well as a legal career. His return to law-firm partnership and the timing of APL counsel’s knowledge became disputed. ([Law Resource](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/032/32.F3d.665.93-9040.1251.html))

The arbitrators found cargo loss while the stevedores handled the goods and treated the charter’s allocation of responsibility as dispositive, without determining a separate delivery point. The award’s detailed calculation totals $135,316.13 including interest; the opinion contains a three-cent inconsistency in its statement of principal. That transcription difference should not be silently treated as a new damages finding. ([Law Resource](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/032/32.F3d.665.93-9040.1251.html))

**Jurisdiction:** Maritime jurisdiction supports the underlying proceeding; the FAA authorizes review of award-confirmation orders, with § 1291 also addressing finality. **Preservation:** Arbitrator qualification, manifest disregard, and the fee sanction are presented. **Additional position:** The Society of Maritime Arbitrators appeared as amicus; its complete brief was not retrieved. **Missing material:** Charter, award, disclosure documents, exact sanction findings, and notice history.

## Law entering the event

**Cutoff:** August 2, 1994. **Simulation holdings:** None supplied.

### Issue 1: Contractual qualification of the arbitrator

**Review and law:** Interpretation of the contractual qualification is legal; disputed historical knowledge is factual. *Kulukundis Shipping v. Amtorg Trading*, 126 F.2d 978 (2d Cir. 1942), and *American Almond Products v. Consolidated Pecan Sales*, 144 F.2d 448 (2d Cir. 1944), inform commercial arbitration’s practical context. No directly controlling Supreme Court holding excludes lawyers from a “commercial men” clause. ([Law Resource](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/032/32.F3d.665.93-9040.1251.html))

**Positions:** APL argues that the phrase excludes a practicing attorney and that the disclosure became misleading. Webster emphasizes Hennessey’s substantial commercial experience and argues waiver from counsel’s knowledge.

**Paths:** Decide qualification from the contractual language and experience. If he qualifies, the disputed waiver question need not be reached. A categorical rule either admitting every lawyer or excluding all lawyers would exceed the necessary issue.

### Issue 2: Manifest disregard

**Test and burden:** Under *Merrill Lynch v. Bobker*, 808 F.2d 930 (2d Cir. 1986), the challenger must show more than an arguable legal error: disregard of governing law known to the arbitrators. Review is not a rehearing of the cargo claim. ([Law Resource](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/032/32.F3d.665.93-9040.1251.html))

**Positions:** APL invokes maritime delivery rules, including *Farrell Lines v. Highlands Insurance*, 696 F.2d 28 (2d Cir. 1982). Webster answers that the charter independently assigned responsibility for stevedores, making the proposed delivery inquiry unnecessary.

**Paths:** Confirm if the award rests on a permissible contractual rationale. Vacate only if the demanding legal standard is met. The court cannot substitute its preferred reading merely because it would analyze delivery differently.

### Issue 3: Sanctions

**Review and law:** The sanction must be assessed under the Rule 11 regime applicable to the district proceedings, not automatically under the December 1993 amendments. *Eastway Construction v. City of New York*, 762 F.2d 243 (2d Cir. 1985), *Business Guides v. Chromatic Communications*, 498 U.S. 533 (1991), and *Cooter & Gell v. Hartmarx*, 496 U.S. 384 (1990), distinguish objectively unjustified filings from unsuccessful but colorable arguments. ([Law Resource](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/032/32.F3d.665.93-9040.1251.html))

**Positions and paths:** Webster regards the challenge as an after-the-award attempt to evade arbitration. APL identifies genuine questions about the contractual phrase and disclosure. Sustain sanctions only on an adequate Rule 11 basis; otherwise vacate the fee award while independently addressing confirmation.
