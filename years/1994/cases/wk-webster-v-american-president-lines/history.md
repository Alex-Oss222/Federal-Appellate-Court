# W.K. Webster & Co. v. American President Lines, Ltd.

**Event:** August 2, 1994. **Panel:** Feinberg, Miner, Knapp. **Author:** Miner; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** The arbitration award was sustained, but the $2,000 fee award was vacated. The court found the arbitrator commercially qualified and rejected manifest disregard; it did not treat APL’s unsuccessful arguments as sanctionable merely because they failed. **Mandate date:** Unverified. **Premise differences:** Miner’s proposed replacement and the disclosed minor monetary transcription discrepancy.
