# Schulz v. Williams

**Historical events:** Operative disposition November 2, 1994; explanatory opinion December 27, 1994. **Panel:** Miner, McLaughlin, Cabranes. **Author:** Cabranes; no separate opinion.

**Disposition and grounds:** The court preserved the injunction. It found appellate standing, held Schulz’s claim precluded without disposing of the remaining plaintiffs on that basis, rejected the constitutional attack on § 6-140, and held the discriminatory free-list arrangement under § 5-602 unlawful. The list-access violation supplied the ground for sustaining the equitable relief. **Mandate:** The operative appellate ruling preceded the election; a separate mandate issuance date was not verified. **Premise differences:** Cabranes’s proposed replacement and explicit correction of the simulation’s operative date. No future election result is used as an earlier premise. ([OpenJurist](https://openjurist.org/44/f3d/48/schulz-v-williams))
