# Schulz v. Williams

**Status:** Version 1; user-approved. **Baseline:** November 2, 1994 packet. **Approval reference:** User instruction of 2026-10-12 approving all eighteen Cycle 001 briefs as written ("all ... approved - change"), subject to each brief’s stated conditions. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Thresholds.**  
Propose recognizing Long’s supported competitive standing, applying claim preclusion to Schulz, and declining to extend preclusion to the remaining plaintiffs without the required party or privity showing. Each conclusion rests on its own doctrine.

**Component: Merits.**  
Propose sustaining the district-information requirements under the received burden framework on the reported record, while invalidating the discriminatory free-list rule under the directly relevant prior authority. The first conclusion should not minimize actual petitioning costs; the second should not silently invalidate every related administrative requirement.

**Component: Remedy.**  
Propose preserving the limited additional petition opportunity insofar as the trial record connects it to the completed list-access injury and establishes meaningful candidate support. State the causal and temporal basis explicitly. Do not treat the remedy as a general exemption from nomination law. This applies Stone’s commitments to actual participation, separate statutory and constitutional sources, and injury-matched election relief.
