# United States v. Johns

**Case:** United States v. Johns, docket 92-1775

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-06-13; 27 F.3d 31 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Vacated the sentence and remanded for resentencing.

**Historical panel:** Frank X. Altimari, Joseph M. McLaughlin, Dennis Jacobs.

**Opinion author and joins:** Dennis Jacobs wrote the opinion. Joined by McLaughlin.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The denial-of-guilt protection in the applicable obstruction commentary covered the unsworn presentence denials at issue, notwithstanding their falsity and materiality. Removing that obstruction basis required renewed consideration of acceptance of responsibility; it did not compel acceptance credit.

**Separate opinions:** Altimari dissented, giving greater force to the materially false-information provision and viewing the statements as punishable deception rather than protected denial.

**Remedy and mandate:** Resentencing without the rejected obstruction rationale and reconsideration of acceptance under the proper independent analysis. The separate mandate’s issuance date and any stay or recall status are not established by an independently supplied docket; they are left unknown.
