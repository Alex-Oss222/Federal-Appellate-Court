## Dependence on the historical judgment

- **Rehearing or certiorari by the United States:** Would depend on the vacatur of the 51-month sentence and the holding that the unsworn presentence denials fall within the denial-of-guilt protection of the application note to U.S.S.G. § 3C1.1. No such filing was found.
- **Rehearing or certiorari by Johns:** Would depend on an adverse result on a remaining issue; the historical judgment was favorable to him on obstruction. No such filing was found.
- **Resentencing and any second appeal:** Depend on the vacatur and remand, including the instruction to reconsider acceptance of responsibility independently. If the court had affirmed, the sentence would have stood and no remand would follow.
- **Dissent feature:** Judge Altimari would have affirmed. A judgment affirming would shift any later review to Johns's side and remove the remand path.
