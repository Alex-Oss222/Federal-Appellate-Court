# United States v. Duncan

**Case:** United States v. Duncan, docket 93-1402

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-12-02; 42 F.3d 97 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Affirmed the convictions and sentence.

**Historical panel:** Wilfred Feinberg, Lawrence W. Pierce, Roger J. Miner.

**Opinion author and joins:** Lawrence W. Pierce wrote the opinion. Joined by Feinberg and Miner.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The court approved Mulligan’s financial-record testimony as permissible assistance and distinguished Scop. It also offered an alternative harmlessness discussion using Civil Rule 61 and civil prejudice authorities. The intended and proved post-enactment bank transactions defeated the ex post facto objection; the specifically identified bank-fraud conspiracy ground sustained Count One independently of the challenged IRS object; the leadership finding was supported.
