# United States v. Duncan

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Pierce wrote for a unanimous panel affirming the convictions and sentence. Judges Feinberg and Miner joined; there was no separate opinion. The opinion held Mulligan's testimony admissible and added an alternative harmlessness discussion that invoked Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 61 and civil authorities concerning prejudice. *Duncan*, 42 F.3d at 101-07.

Stone's proposed result matches the historical judgment but would not reproduce that civil-rule formulation in a criminal appeal. He would also state the limitation on *Griffin* expressly and ground the conspiracy disposition in the actual verdict rather than a sweeping presumption about general verdicts.
