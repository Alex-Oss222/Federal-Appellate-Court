## Historical comparison extract

On 1994-08-08 the Second Circuit vacated the summary judgment for the insurer and remanded. McCostis v. Home Insurance Co. of Indiana, No. 93-9283, 31 F.3d 110, 112-13 (2d Cir. 1994). The panel was Pratt, Walker and Constance Baker Motley. Walker wrote, joined by Pratt, and Motley dissented briefly and would have affirmed for the district court's reasons. The court found the fee-return exclusion open to more than one reasonable reading where the demand exceeded what McCostis personally received, and required consideration of extrinsic evidence of intended meaning, then construction against the insurer if that evidence did not resolve it. The simulated judgment is unchanged: vacatur and remand, Walker writing. The lineup differs: Stone-Khoury takes Motley's seat, so the vote is 3-0 with no dissent. The reasoning follows the historical ground.
