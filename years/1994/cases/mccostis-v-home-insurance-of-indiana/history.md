# McCostis v. Home Insurance Co. of Indiana

**Case:** McCostis v. Home Insurance Co. of Indiana, docket 93-9283

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-08-08; 31 F.3d 110 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Vacated the judgment for the insurer and remanded.

**Historical panel:** George C. Pratt, John M. Walker, Jr., Constance Baker Motley.

**Opinion author and joins:** John M. Walker, Jr. wrote the opinion. Joined by Pratt.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The exclusion concerning return or restitution of legal fees admitted more than one reasonable reading in this overbilling arrangement, where the underlying demand sought amounts beyond what McCostis personally received and was framed through broader liability theories. The court required consideration of extrinsic evidence of intended meaning, followed if necessary by construction against the insurer. It did not conclusively award indemnity or decide all settlement allocation.

**Separate opinions:** Motley dissented briefly and would have affirmed for the reasons given by the district court.

**Remedy and mandate:** Extrinsic-evidence inquiry on the disputed exclusion and application of the appropriate ambiguity rule if that evidence did not resolve meaning.
