# McCostis v. Home Insurance Co. of Indiana

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Walker wrote for himself and Judge Pratt. The court vacated and remanded for the extrinsic-evidence inquiry and, if necessary, construction against the insurer. Judge Motley dissented briefly and would have affirmed for the district court's reasons. *McCostis*, 31 F.3d at 112-13.

Stone's proposed disposition substantially tracks that result. His emphasis is the difference between defense and indemnity, the specific ambiguity concerning money received by others, and the obligation to follow New York's interpretive sequence rather than select an intuitively fair insurance outcome.
