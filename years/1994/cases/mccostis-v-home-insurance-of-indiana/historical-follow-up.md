## Dependence on the historical judgment

No later event is verified, so none can be tied to a feature of the judgment. If one is later found, test it against these features. The insurer was the party aggrieved by the vacatur and by the remand for extrinsic evidence on the "return or restitution of legal fees" exclusion, with contra-insurer construction if the evidence does not resolve meaning; McCostis was the party aggrieved by the summary judgment and would have been the party seeking review had the judgment been affirmed. The vacatur and remand was interlocutory and returned the case to the district court, so any later Second Circuit or Supreme Court event would follow a new district-court ruling rather than the 1994 judgment itself. A differently decided Second Circuit judgment, such as an affirmance, would end the 1994 appeal and leave no remand to track.
