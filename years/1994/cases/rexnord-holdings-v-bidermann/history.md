# Rexnord Holdings, Inc. v. Bidermann

**Event:** April 7, 1994. **Panel:** Miner, Mahoney, Restani. **Author:** Miner; no separate opinion.

**Disposition and holding:** Affirmed. The contract defenses did not establish a genuine material dispute. The clerk’s entry was ministerial because the judicial function had been completed before bankruptcy. **Remedy/mandate:** Money judgment left intact; mandate issuance date not verified. **Premise differences:** Mahoney’s proposed replacement only, subject to Stone’s qualification.
