# Rexnord Holdings, Inc. v. Bidermann

**Status:** Version 1; user-approved. **Cutoff:** April 7, 1994. **Approval reference:** User instruction of 2026-10-12 approving all eighteen Cycle 001 briefs as written ("all ... approved - change"), subject to each brief’s stated conditions. **Conditions:** Qualification by the January submission. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Contract judgment.**  
Under § 1291 and de novo review, propose affirmance. Apply the actual contract and Rule 56 without favoring either private party. The admitted default, cumulative-remedies language, and absence of evidentiary support for the defenses support enforcement. The implied covenant should not supply a new obligation to extend credit.

**Component: Stay.**  
Propose affirmance limited to clerical entry of a fully completed prepetition judicial decision. The legal relationship is resolution of the ministerial-act question within § 362 and binding circuit stay law. Reserve all postpetition collection and any case requiring further judicial discretion. Stone’s ordinary-law and remedial commitments support that distinction, not a general creditor exception.
