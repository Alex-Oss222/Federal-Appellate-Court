# Romero-Morales v. INS

**Event:** May 27, 1994. **Panel:** Feinberg, Miner, Knapp. **Author:** Feinberg; no separate opinion.

**Disposition and grounds:** The petition was granted and the matter remanded because the agency had not adequately considered the circumstances surrounding nonappearance. The court did not grant asylum or itself conclusively find exceptional circumstances. **Mandate date:** Unverified. **Premise differences:** Proposed replacement of Miner only.
