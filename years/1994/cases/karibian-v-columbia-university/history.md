# Karibian v. Columbia University

**Historical event:** January 25, 1994. **Historical panel:** Mahoney, McLaughlin, and Heaney. **Author and joins:** McLaughlin wrote for the panel; no separate opinion appears.

**Disposition and grounds:** The court reversed summary judgment. Submission to supervisory sexual demands could support quid pro quo liability without proof of economic loss, and employer responsibility could arise where supervisory authority facilitated the harassment. The state claims were reinstated for further proceedings. The court did not find the disputed coercion proved or award damages. **Mandate:** Remand ordered; issuance date not independently established. **Known premise differences:** The simulation proposes a 1994 resubmission and replaces McLaughlin. No other factual departure is proposed. ([Law Resource](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/014/14.F3d.773.93-7188.513.html))
