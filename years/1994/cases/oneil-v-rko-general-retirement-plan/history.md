# O’Neil v. Retirement Plan for Salaried Employees of RKO General, Inc.

**Event:** September 26, 1994. **Panel:** Oakes, Meskill, Altimari. **Author:** Altimari; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** Affirmed dismissal, sustaining the exclusion under deferential review because the plans delegated interpretive discretion and the interpretation was permissible. The court did not require acceptance of every reason advanced by the administrators. **Mandate date:** Unverified. **Premise differences:** Altimari’s proposed replacement only.
