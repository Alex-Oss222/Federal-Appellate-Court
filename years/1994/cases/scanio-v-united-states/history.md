# Scanio v. United States

**Case:** Scanio v. United States, docket 94-2002

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-10-11; 37 F.3d 858 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Affirmed dismissal of the § 2255 motion for lack of custody at filing.

**Historical panel:** Ellsworth A. Van Graafeiland, Lawrence W. Pierce, Frank X. Altimari.

**Opinion author and joins:** Frank X. Altimari wrote the opinion. Joined by Van Graafeiland.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The majority treated the supervised-release term as expired before the November 29, 1993 filing. It held that Rule 6(a)’s computation provisions could not extend custody or enlarge § 2255 jurisdiction and that collateral consequences did not satisfy the initial custody requirement.

**Separate opinions:** Pierce dissented, questioning the custody calculation and the rejection of the holiday/closure computation principle. The disagreement concerned access to the § 2255 forum, not an appellate determination of the underlying ineffective-assistance claim.

**Remedy and mandate:** Dismissal remained in place; no hearing on the collateral merits was ordered. The separate mandate’s issuance date and any stay or recall status are not established by an independently supplied docket; they are left unknown.
