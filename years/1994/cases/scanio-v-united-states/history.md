# Scanio v. United States

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Altimari wrote the opinion, joined by Judge Van Graafeiland, affirming dismissal because custody had expired before the November 29 filing and Rule 6(a) could not alter that status. Judge Pierce dissented, challenging the custody determination and the rejection of the holiday-computation principle. *Scanio*, 37 F.3d at 858-65.

Stone's proposed remand shares the concern about court-created loss of access but does not simply adopt an extension of custody through Rule 6(a). The constructive-lodging approach is a newly proposed theory requiring approval, briefing, and factual support, not a historical holding or an established simulation result.
