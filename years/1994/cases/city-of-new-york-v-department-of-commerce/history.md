# City of New York v. United States Department of Commerce

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Kearse wrote the historical opinion, joined by Judge Leval. The court vacated and remanded, holding that the government must justify the refusal to adjust as essential to a legitimate objective after the plaintiffs' showing. Judge Timbers dissented and would have affirmed on Judge McLaughlin's opinion, also noting contrary Sixth and Seventh Circuit decisions. *City of New York*, 34 F.3d at 1129-31.

Stone's proposed judgment also remands, but his rationale materially differs. He does not equate disparate undercount with racial discrimination or automatically import *Karcher*'s burden shifting. His remand preserves plaintiffs' burden and genuine technical discretion while requiring an independently stated constitutional judgment. No later Supreme Court disposition supplies the law for this simulation.
