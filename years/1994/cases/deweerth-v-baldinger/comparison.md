## Historical comparison extract

On October 27, 1994, a Second Circuit panel of Judges Altimari, Walker and Owen filed an amended opinion by Judge Walker, joined by Judge Altimari. It reversed the Rule 60(b) order and restored the earlier final judgment. The majority separated the district court's authority to consider later events from satisfaction of Rule 60, held that the later authoritative New York decision did not supply extraordinary circumstances to reopen this final diversity judgment, and held that continued possession was a consequence of the adjudication, not prospective operation under Rule 60(b)(5). Judge Owen dissented, stressing the district judge's equitable discretion. The simulated judgment is unchanged: reversal and reinstatement on the same grounds. Only the lineup differs, with Judge Stone-Khoury in Owen's place and joining Walker, making the result 3-0 with no dissent. Source: DeWeerth v. Baldinger, 38 F.3d 1266, 1271-78 (2d Cir. 1994), Nos. 93-7144, 93-7146.
