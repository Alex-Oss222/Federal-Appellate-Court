# DeWeerth v. Baldinger

**Case:** DeWeerth v. Baldinger, docket 93-7144, 93-7146

**Historical event and date:** Amended panel disposition on 1994-10-27; 38 F.3d 1266 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Reversed the order granting Rule 60 relief and the resulting reopened judgment.

**Historical panel:** Frank X. Altimari, John M. Walker, Jr., Richard Owen.

**Opinion author and joins:** John M. Walker, Jr. wrote the opinion. Joined by Altimari.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The amended opinion separated the district court’s authority to consider later events from satisfaction of Rule 60. The subsequent authoritative state decision did not supply extraordinary circumstances sufficient to reopen this final diversity judgment. Continued possession was a consequence of the adjudication, not prospective operation of an executory decree within Rule 60(b)(5).

**Separate opinions:** Owen dissented, emphasizing the district judge’s equitable discretion, New York’s express rejection of the federal prediction, and the injustice of preserving the contrary federal result.
