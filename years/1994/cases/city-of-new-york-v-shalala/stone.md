# City of New York v. Shalala

**Status:** Version 1; user-approved. **Baseline:** September 13, 1994. **Approval reference:** User instruction of 2026-10-12 approving all eighteen Cycle 001 briefs as written ("all ... approved - change"), subject to each brief’s stated conditions. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Year 16.**  
Propose reversing the district judgment setting aside the disallowance. Audit closure did not forgive unresolved receivables, and unverified internal offsets did not satisfy the applicable accounting requirements.

**Component: Year 19.**  
Propose vacatur and remand for agency reconsideration of the independently verified offsets. Preserve lawful inquiry into continuing obligations, but reject revival of expired document-retention duties and the unsupported assumption that offsets erase unrelated repayment obligations. Leave genuinely unresolved annual-accounting questions to the agency in the first instance.

**Component: Interest.**  
Recognize common-law authority under *Texas* and remand for the required assessment on year 16. Do not affirm a specific rate without that work. This applies the historical administrative-review regime; Stone’s preferred deference framework is not substituted for governing law.
