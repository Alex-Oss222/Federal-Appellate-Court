# City of New York v. Shalala

**Event:** September 13, 1994. **Panel:** Walker, Jacobs, Daly. **Author:** Walker; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** Reversed the judgment overturning the year-16 disallowance and remanded its interest issue. Vacated the judgment concerning year 19 and remanded for further agency proceedings on offsets. Each side was to bear its own costs. **Mandate date:** Not independently established. **Premise differences:** Jacobs’s proposed replacement only; the distinction between unaudited and independently audited offsets is preserved. ([Justia Law](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/34/1161/552099/))
