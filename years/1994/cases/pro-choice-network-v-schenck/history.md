# Pro-Choice Network of Western New York v. Schenck

**Case:** Pro-Choice Network of Western New York v. Schenck, docket 92-7302, 93-7918

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-09-06; 67 F.3d 359 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Affirmed in part and reversed in part the preliminary injunction; affirmed refusal to dissolve it wholesale after the federal claim was dismissed.

**Historical panel:** James L. Oakes, Thomas J. Meskill, Frank X. Altimari.

**Opinion author and joins:** Thomas J. Meskill wrote the opinion. Joined by Altimari.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The court retained the developed pendent state-law litigation after Bray. Applying Madsen, it rejected the fifteen-foot restrictions and the requirement that sidewalk counselors cease their speech on a listener’s demand while sustaining independently lawful access, trespass, and noise protections. The injunction was assessed provision by provision rather than as an all-or-nothing restraint.

**Separate opinions:** Oakes dissented from invalidating the challenged protective provisions, emphasizing the record of obstruction, the practical protection of patients, and the district court’s remedial judgment.

**Remedy and mandate:** Partial invalidation and further proceedings consistent with the surviving terms. This entry concerns the September 6, 1994 panel event, not a later en banc proceeding. The separate mandate’s issuance date and any stay or recall status are not established by an independently supplied docket; they are left unknown.
