# Pro-Choice Network of Western New York v. Schenck

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Meskill wrote the historical majority opinion, joined by Judge Altimari. The court affirmed in part and reversed in part the injunction, invalidating the bubble-zone and cease-and-desist provisions, while affirming the refusal to vacate the injunction wholesale. Judge Oakes dissented from striking the disputed restrictions, emphasizing repeated violations and their effect on access. *Pro-Choice Network*, 67 F.3d at 367-76.

Stone's proposed disposition largely shares the majority's speech concerns but avoids turning prior failure of an injunction into a categorical prerequisite. It expressly separates fixed and moving restrictions and preserves the possibility of a supported entrance-access remedy. No simulated colleague's agreement is inferred from either historical opinion, and the later en banc and Supreme Court decisions are not applied.
