# Hathaway v. Coughlin

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Altimari wrote the opinion, joined by Judge McLaughlin, affirming the denial of Foote's motion. Judge Jacobs dissented, emphasizing the treatment supplied, the specialists' judgments, and the distinction between medical disagreement and deliberate indifference. *Hathaway*, 37 F.3d at 66-70.

Stone's proposed judgment matches the historical disposition but is limited to the sufficiency of a plaintiff-favorable account and the legal immunity question. It does not reproduce the historical judges' votes in the simulation or resolve the factual dispute against Foote.
