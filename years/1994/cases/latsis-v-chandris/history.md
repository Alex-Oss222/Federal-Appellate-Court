# Latsis v. Chandris, Inc.

## Historical disposition and comparison

**Historical result:** judgment vacated and case remanded for a new trial. **Author:** Oakes. **Join:** Cardamone. **Separate opinion:** Kearse dissented, disputing the basis for reversal under the preservation and drydock issues. Stone's proposed judgment is the same, but his proposed rationale concentrates on the preserved error and does not automatically adopt every broader formulation in the historical majority. *Latsis*, 20 F.3d at 50-57.
