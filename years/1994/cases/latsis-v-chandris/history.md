# Latsis v. Chandris, Inc.

**Case:** Latsis v. Chandris, Inc., docket 93-7704

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-03-24; 20 F.3d 45 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Vacated the defense judgment and remanded for a new trial.

**Historical panel:** James L. Oakes, Amalya L. Kearse, Richard J. Cardamone.

**Opinion author and joins:** James L. Oakes wrote the opinion. Joined by Cardamone.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The majority found the seaman-status charge erroneous in its treatment of the employment relationship and the categorical exclusion of the drydock period. It treated the challenged exclusion and the broader instructional framework under the applicable preservation analysis, rather than itself resolving disputed vessel-time facts or entering judgment that Latsis was a seaman.

**Separate opinions:** Kearse dissented, contesting reversal under the preservation rules and disagreeing with the majority’s treatment of the drydock/navigation issue.
