# Ayeni v. Mottola

**Status:** Version 1; proposed, not approved. **Baseline:** September 12, 1994 packet, with March 1992 immunity notice. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Media intrusion.**  
Propose affirmance of the denial of qualified-immunity dismissal. Apply existing home-search and warrant-scope law to nonassisting outsiders invited for an unrelated broadcasting purpose. The proposed holding would not depend on a new statutory damages action or a rule requiring a prior case with a television crew.

**Component: Other Fourth Amendment allegations.**  
Permit factual development concerning unnecessary force, damage, prewarrant searching, detention, and Mottola’s participation. Reserve lawful seizure of an identification photograph and reasonable measures genuinely serving execution. The remedy is continued adjudication, not an immediate damages judgment.

**Component: Due process.**  
Do not sustain a duplicative substantive-due-process theory where the Fourth Amendment governs. This separates the source of the right from immunity and remedy, consistent with Stone’s instructions to protect the home while applying the actual qualified-immunity standard.
