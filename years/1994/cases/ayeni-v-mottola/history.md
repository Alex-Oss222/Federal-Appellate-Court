# Ayeni v. Mottola

**Event:** September 12, 1994. **Panel:** Newman, Pierce, Leval. **Author:** Newman; no separate opinion.

**Disposition and grounds:** Affirmed the order denying qualified-immunity dismissal. The Fourth Amendment allegations were sufficient, including unauthorized media intrusion and potentially excessive execution. Questions concerning prewarrant activity and Mottola’s involvement remained factual. The court rejected the substantive-due-process contention and recognized that a photograph could lawfully be seized for identification. **Mandate date:** Unverified. **Premise differences:** Leval’s proposed replacement only; no later media-search or Bivens decision is incorporated.
