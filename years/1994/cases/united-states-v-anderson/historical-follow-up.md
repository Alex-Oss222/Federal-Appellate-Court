## Dependence on the historical judgment

- **No admitted event depends on the judgment.** If Anderson had petitioned, the petition would have come from an affirmance of the seventeen-month revocation term. The relevant features would be the holdings that the Chapter Seven policy statements are advisory and that rehabilitation considerations may support the choice of the revocation prison term, over Judge Kearse's dissent invoking 18 U.S.C. § 3582(a). A judgment vacating the sentence and ordering resentencing would give Anderson no adverse judgment to challenge. A judgment affirming on a different ground would still permit a petition from Anderson on the same question.
- **Candidate No. 94-5768.** If it were ever shown to be Anderson's, it would depend on the same affirmance. It is not so shown.
