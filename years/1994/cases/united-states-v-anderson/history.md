# United States v. Anderson

**Case:** United States v. Anderson, docket 93-1097

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-02-17; 15 F.3d 278 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Affirmed the seventeen-month revocation sentence.

**Historical panel:** James L. Oakes, Amalya L. Kearse, Frank X. Altimari.

**Opinion author and joins:** James L. Oakes wrote the opinion. Joined by Altimari.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The majority treated Chapter Seven’s revocation provisions as advisory policy statements and held that the statutory treatment and rehabilitation considerations could support selection of the revocation prison term. It distinguished the framework governing revocation from the prohibition invoked concerning an initial decision to impose imprisonment. The term remained within the statutory ceiling.

**Separate opinions:** Kearse dissented from allowing rehabilitation to determine imprisonment following revocation, reading the statutory prohibition and related sentencing provisions together. Her objection did not transform the Chapter Seven recommendation into a binding ceiling.

**Remedy and mandate:** No resentencing; the revocation judgment was affirmed. The separate mandate’s issuance date and any stay or recall status are not established by an independently supplied docket; they are left unknown.
