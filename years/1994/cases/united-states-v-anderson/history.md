# United States v. Anderson

## Historical disposition and comparison

**Historical result:** affirmed. **Author:** Oakes. **Join:** Altimari. **Separate opinion:** Kearse dissented from permitting rehabilitation-based imprisonment following revocation. Stone's proposed statutory construction aligns with that concern and differs from the historical judgment. His agreement that Chapter Seven is advisory does not imply approval of the sentence's rehabilitative basis. *Anderson*, 15 F.3d at 281-86.
