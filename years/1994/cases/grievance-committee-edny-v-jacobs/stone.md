# Grievance Committee for the Eastern District of New York v. Jacobs

**Status:** Version 1; proposed, not approved. **Baseline:** December 23, 1994. **Approval reference:** Preparation request. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Jurisdiction and reciprocal discipline.**  
Propose an express jurisdictional determination followed by affirmance of the federal suspension. Apply *Selling* and the local rule as an independent federal inquiry, not as automatic enforcement of the state court’s result. The reported hearings and federal opportunities to identify defects do not establish a material reason to repeat the state evidentiary trial.

**Component: Procedural limits.**  
Reject the demand for another hearing absent a concrete, decision-material factual issue that the existing record cannot resolve. Reserve genuinely new evidence, a materially deficient state process, and claims outside the appeal. Do not use broad statements about discipline being noncriminal to extinguish protections that apply on their own terms. Stone’s commitments require meaningful process and a lawful forum, not unlimited repetition of adjudication.
