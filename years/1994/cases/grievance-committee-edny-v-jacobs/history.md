# Grievance Committee for the Eastern District of New York v. Jacobs

**Event:** December 23, 1994. **Panel:** Feinberg, Meskill, Mahoney. **Author:** Feinberg; no separate opinion.

**Disposition and grounds:** Affirmed the federal reciprocal suspension. The court found the procedures adequate, rejected a claimed entitlement to another evidentiary trial on the showing made, and found no established basis under the reciprocal-discipline criteria to depart from the state sanction. **Remedy/mandate:** The federal suspension remained in place; mandate issuance date was not independently established. **Known premise differences:** Mahoney’s proposed replacement and the disclosed submission/argument documentation gap.
