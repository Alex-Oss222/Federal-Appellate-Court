# United States v. Knoll

**Event:** February 14, 1994. **Panel:** Cardamone, Mahoney, and Cedarbaum. **Author:** Cardamone; no separate opinion appears.

**Disposition:** The court reversed the count-four convictions as time-barred, remanded Knoll’s remaining case for findings concerning governmental participation in the private search, and otherwise rejected the challenges, including Gleave’s challenge to count three. The remand did not itself establish suppression or require a new trial. **Mandate date:** Not independently established. **Premise differences:** Proposed 1994 resubmission and replacement of Mahoney; no factual alteration.
