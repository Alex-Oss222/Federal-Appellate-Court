# In re Secured Equipment Trust of Eastern Air Lines, Inc.

**Case:** In re Secured Equipment Trust of Eastern Air Lines, Inc., docket 94-5015, 94-5017

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-10-17; 38 F.3d 86 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Affirmed dismissal of the involuntary proceeding against the Trust.

**Historical panel:** Amalya L. Kearse, Frank X. Altimari, Allen G. Schwartz.

**Opinion author and joins:** Frank X. Altimari wrote the opinion. Joined by Schwartz.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The majority classified the Trust as an arrangement preserving collateral for Eastern’s secured financing rather than a business trust eligible under the Code. It treated the substantial post-default operations as incidental to that protective purpose. It expressly declined to establish profit generation as a universal prerequisite for every business trust.

**Separate opinions:** Kearse dissented, stressing the certificated investment structure, authorized commercial activities, actual equipment operations, and continuity with the predecessor Bankruptcy Act.

**Remedy and mandate:** The eligibility dismissal remained in place. The decision did not establish the remaining conditions for an order for relief or redistribute priorities. The separate mandate’s issuance date and any stay or recall status are not established by an independently supplied docket; they are left unknown.
