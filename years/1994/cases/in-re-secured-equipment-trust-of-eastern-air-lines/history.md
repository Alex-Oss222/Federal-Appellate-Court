# In re Secured Equipment Trust of Eastern Air Lines, Inc.

## Historical disposition and comparison

Judge Altimari wrote the opinion, joined by Judge Schwartz, affirming dismissal. The majority emphasized the Trust's collateral-preservation purpose and treated its business activities as incidental, while expressly declining to make profit generation universally necessary. Judge Kearse dissented, relying on the investment structure, actual operations, and continuity with the former Bankruptcy Act. *Secured Equipment Trust*, 38 F.3d at 89-93.

Stone's proposed judgment differs from the historical result and overlaps Kearse's statutory analysis. It should not misdescribe the majority as having categorically barred every nonprofit trust. His disagreement concerns the classification of this commercial financing arrangement, not an assumed power to use bankruptcy whenever another forum is inconvenient.
