# General Electric Capital Corp. v. Eva Armadora, S.A.

**Event:** September 22, 1994. **Panel:** Feinberg, Van Graafeiland, Winter. **Author and joins:** Winter wrote; Feinberg joined. Van Graafeiland concurred in the result without a separate substantive explanation in the retrieved opinion.

**Disposition:** Reversed on equitable estoppel and remanded for the resulting relief, including recalculation of attorney’s fees. The borrowers could not invoke a contrary interpretation after inducing consent on GECC’s stated understanding. **Mandate date:** Unverified. **Premise differences:** Winter’s proposed replacement; no new panel alignment is inferred.
