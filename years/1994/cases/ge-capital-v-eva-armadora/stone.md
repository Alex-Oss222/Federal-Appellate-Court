# General Electric Capital Corp. v. Eva Armadora, S.A.

**Status:** Version 1; proposed, not approved. **Baseline:** September 22, 1994. **Approval reference:** Preparation only. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Estoppel.**  
Propose reversal on the narrow refinancing estoppel ground. GECC clearly stated the condition underlying its consent; Armadora’s cover letter represented that the draft formalized that understanding without directly disclosing the materially different calculation later asserted. The proposed rule applies New York estoppel to induced reliance, not Stone’s own preferred contract policy.

**Component: Remedy.**  
Require application of the October 27 calculation to the refinancing and remand the resulting accounting, interest, and reasonable contractual fees as necessary. Reserve the meaning of similar clauses in transactions lacking these communications. Stone’s jurisprudence requires prediction and application of state law, not replacement of it with generalized fairness.
