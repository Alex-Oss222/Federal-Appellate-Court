# McLee v. Chrysler Corp.

**Event:** October 12, 1994. **Panel:** Newman, Kearse, Cardamone. **Author:** Newman; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** Mandamus issued directing arrangements for reassignment under normal district procedures. Permission for interlocutory appeal was denied without prejudice, and the stay was lifted. The court did not decide the employment-discrimination merits or grant summary judgment. **Mandate date:** Not independently verified. **Premise differences:** Cardamone’s proposed replacement; the earlier September procedural order remains preserved.
