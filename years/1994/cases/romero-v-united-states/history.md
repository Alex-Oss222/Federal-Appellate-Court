# Romero v. United States

**Event:** June 29, 1994. **Panel:** Feinberg, Mahoney, Kram. **Writing:** Per curiam; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** Affirmed denial of relief while holding the newly discovered evidence motion timely under Rule 33. The court treated the published clarification as sufficient and denied the separate record-correction motion as moot. It also accepted the district court’s rejection of the remaining claims. **Mandate:** Issuance date not established. **Premise differences:** Mahoney’s proposed replacement; the June 17/June 18 lower-order discrepancy remains unresolved rather than silently corrected.
