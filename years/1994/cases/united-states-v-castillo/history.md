# United States v. Castillo

## Historical disposition and comparison

**Historical result:** affirmed. **Author:** Mahoney. **Join:** Oakes. **Separate opinion:** Lumbard dissented, principally disputing the admission and harmlessness of the repeated statements. The proposed Stone disposition differs from the historical judgment and substantially overlaps the dissent's evidentiary concern, but does not assume Lumbard would join Stone's full treatment of testimony, preservation, or sentencing. No simulated vote is established. *Castillo*, 14 F.3d at 802-12.
