# Goldman v. Commissioner of Internal Revenue

**Event:** November 2, 1994. **Panel:** Meskill, Mahoney, Walker. **Author:** Meskill; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** Affirmed the Tax Court judgment. The settlement did not cover 1982, and the negligence findings were sustained. The opinion notes that enhanced interest was not contested on appeal and that the § 6659 addition had been conceded below. **Mandate date:** Unverified. **Premise differences:** Walker’s proposed replacement; the source’s investment-date inconsistency is not silently resolved.
