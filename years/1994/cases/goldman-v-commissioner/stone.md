# Goldman v. Commissioner of Internal Revenue

**Status:** Version 1; proposed, not approved. **Baseline:** November 2, 1994. **Approval reference:** Preparation request. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Settlement.**  
Propose affirmance. Apply the settlement’s actual terms and the official’s authority, not a generalized preference against tax liability. An agreement resolving 1981 did not eliminate 1982 liability.

**Component: Negligence.**  
Propose affirmance under clear-error review. The advertised deduction ratio, unproven technology, conflicted sales advice, and absence of independent expertise support the finding. Reserve cases involving competent, independent investigation or materially different advice. Do not treat *Boyle* as abolishing reliance on substantive tax advice. The proposal follows Stone’s ordinary statutory and review principles, without an extra tax-policy overlay.
