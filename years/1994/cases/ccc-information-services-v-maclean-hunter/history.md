# CCC Information Services, Inc. v. Maclean Hunter Market Reports, Inc.

**Event:** December 5, 1994. **Panel:** Kearse, Pierce, Leval. **Author:** Leval; no separate opinion.

**Disposition and grounds:** The court reversed, held that Maclean had demonstrated a valid copyright and infringement, directed entry of judgment in Maclean’s favor, and remanded for further proceedings. It rejected the district court’s originality, merger, and public-domain reasoning. The opinion’s conclusion is broader than a remand merely to reconsider protectability. **Mandate date:** Unverified. **Known differences:** Leval’s proposed replacement; the Stone proposal expressly reserves the liability-directive question because the retrieved record does not separately establish disposition of every additional pleaded defense.
