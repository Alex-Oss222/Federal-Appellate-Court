# CCC Information Services, Inc. v. Maclean Hunter Market Reports, Inc.

**Status:** Version 1; proposed, not approved. **Baseline:** December 5, 1994. **Approval reference:** Preparation request. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Protectability and challenged defenses.**  
Propose reversal of CCC’s summary judgment. Apply *Feist* and *Kregos* to the original evaluative and compilation contributions actually copied. Reject a blanket public-domain rule triggered by the regulatory references shown here. Reserve facts, independent estimates, valuation methods, limited copying, and materially different incorporation arrangements.

**Component: Remedial scope.**  
Propose remand rather than an unconditional final infringement directive unless the complete appellate record establishes that the additional defenses are resolved, abandoned, or legally immaterial. This is a limit on the present proposal, not an assertion that a particular defense will succeed. Apply *Campbell* to any preserved fair-use issue without treating commercial use or a rejected license request as an automatic answer. Stone’s jurisprudence supplies ordinary copyright and appellate-remedy discipline, not an independent preference for either database access or proprietary control.
