## Supreme Court merits decision, if any

None. Certiorari was denied without opinion. A denial is not approval of the Second Circuit's reasoning. No rehearing entry for No. 94-540 appears in the volume 513 to 515 tables of case names.

## Dependence on the historical judgment

- **Certiorari petition (No. 94-540):** Sought by the defendants, the parties who lost the appeal. It addressed relief from the dismissal for want of appellate jurisdiction, which rested on holding the without-prejudice denial of qualified immunity not conclusive under the collateral-order doctrine. It depends on a judgment adverse to defendants on appellate jurisdiction. A judgment that took jurisdiction and ruled for defendants (the Walker dissent position) would leave defendants no petition; one ruling for plaintiffs would reverse the posture.
- **Discovery, renewed motion and 1996 judgment:** Depend on the dismissal leaving the district court's provisional order in force and immunity undecided. A merits ruling on immunity in 1994 would have displaced the renewed-motion path.
- **1996 notice of appeal and 1997 affirmance:** Depend on a final district judgment produced by that renewed motion, and are Second Circuit events in the same litigation.
