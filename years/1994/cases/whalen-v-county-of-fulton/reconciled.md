## Thomas J. Meskill (Senior Circuit Judge)

**Reconciled disposition:** Dismiss the individual officials' appeal for want of appellate jurisdiction, without reaching immunity. This is the alternative path the commitment identified, now operative.

**Ground:** The order denied summary judgment without prejudice and permitted renewal after discovery. It is not a sufficiently conclusive determination of the immunity defense under *Cohen*, *Coopers & Lybrand*, and *Mitchell*, applied through *Cartier* and *Golino*. Jurisdiction is decided de novo and first. The conclusion must rest on what the order actually did, not on the words "without prejudice" alone. The merits view in the commitment (no clearly established right, assuming without deciding a protected interest) is not reached and is not a holding.

**Remedy:** Dismiss the appeal in full. The district court's order remains in force and discovery and renewed immunity consideration proceed below. No direction to enter summary judgment. The counties, departments, and state-law claims are unaffected. Nothing in history.md decides whether a later appeal lies from a renewed immunity ruling.

**Join limits:** The four commitment limits stand. He would not join a rationale that (a) holds sibling or extended-family bonds can never carry a protected interest; (b) takes jurisdiction because immunity is invoked or reviews discovery sequencing as such; (c) dismisses on the label without examining the order; or (d) reaches the institutional defendants without a pendent-jurisdiction basis. Added by reconciliation: he would not join a rationale that finds jurisdiction over this order or reaches the immunity merits.

## John M. Walker, Jr. (Active Circuit Judge)

**Reconciled disposition:** Take limited jurisdiction over the legal immunity question and reverse in the reviewable part.

**Ground:** Under *Soares*, review is proper where the defense may be established as a matter of law on the facts viewed for the Whalens (or on fundamental legal error), citing *Cartier*, *Mitchell*, and *Washington Square Post* n.1. On the pleaded facts, with no custodial relationship alleged, no clearly established right of adoptive parents to the sister's placement existed (*Harlow*, *Siegert*, *Smith v. OFFER*, *Rivera*). He would resolve this on the clearly-established prong without deciding whether a protected interest exists (*Easton*, *Severino*), canvassing all relevant precedent (*Elder*).

**Remedy:** Reverse in the reviewable part and remand for entry of summary judgment for the individual defendants on the federal claims resting on the asserted sibling-placement right. Leave the remainder, the counties, and the state-law claims to the district court. History's phrase "granted qualified immunity" and the commitment's "reverse and remand for entry of summary judgment" describe the same substance. History.md gives no more detail on the mechanics, so the commitment's wording is kept.

**Join limits:** The commitment limits stand: no jurisdiction without first finding the defense decidable as a matter of law or fundamental error; no reliance on unalleged facts or resolution of disputed facts; no broad constitutional rule on sibling association. Added by reconciliation: he would not join a dismissal on the ground that this order is a provisional deferral requiring factual development.

**Basis for any departure from the commitment or from history:** None. His primary branch matches the historical dissent.
