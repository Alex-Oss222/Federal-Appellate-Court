# Whalen v. County of Fulton

**Case:** Whalen v. County of Fulton, docket 93-7457

**Historical event and date:** Panel disposition on 1994-03-29; 19 F.3d 828 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Dismissed the interlocutory appeal for lack of appellate jurisdiction.

**Historical panel:** Thomas J. Meskill, John M. Walker, Jr., Constance Baker Motley.

**Opinion author and joins:** Thomas J. Meskill wrote the opinion. Joined by Motley.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The denial without prejudice allowed renewed consideration after discovery and was not a sufficiently conclusive qualified-immunity determination under the collateral-order doctrine. The majority did not decide the ultimate constitutional or immunity merits.

**Separate opinions:** Walker dissented. He would have exercised jurisdiction over the legal immunity issue and granted qualified immunity because the asserted right was not clearly established.
