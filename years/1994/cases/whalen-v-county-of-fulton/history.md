# Whalen v. County of Fulton

## Historical disposition and comparison

**Historical result:** appeal dismissed for lack of jurisdiction. **Author:** Meskill. **Join:** Motley. **Separate opinion:** Walker dissented, would have exercised jurisdiction, and would have granted the officials qualified immunity. Stone's proposed disposition agrees with the historical judgment but preserves a functional limit against using nominal deferrals to nullify immunity. It does not establish either remaining judge's simulated vote. *Whalen*, 19 F.3d at 830-34.
