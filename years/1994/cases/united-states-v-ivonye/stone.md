# United States v. Ivonye

**Status:** Version 1; proposed, not approved. **Baseline:** July 8, 1994. **Approval reference:** Preparation only. **Approved fallback/separate writing:** None.

**Component: Guideline calculation.**  
Propose affirmance of the actual-drug calculation under § 3742. Apply binding circuit law to personally imported drugs rather than using a foreseeability limitation designed for another person’s conduct.

**Component: Departure.**  
Decline to review the informed discretionary refusal. Preserve the recognized authority to depart in a genuinely extraordinary case; do not convert that possibility into an entitlement here. Stone’s jurisprudence expressly treats the Guidelines as binding law during this period and distinguishes legal error from discretionary disagreement.
