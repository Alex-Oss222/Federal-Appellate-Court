# United States v. Ivonye

**Event:** July 8, 1994. **Panel:** Newman, Pierce, Leval. **Author:** Newman; no separate opinion.

**Disposition:** Affirmed. The actual heroin governed the calculation, and the district court’s knowing refusal to depart did not supply a reviewable sentencing error. **Mandate date:** Not independently established. **Premise differences:** Leval’s proposed replacement only. ([Justia Law](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/30/275/471763/))
