# American Geophysical Union v. Texaco, Inc.

**Case:** American Geophysical Union v. Texaco, Inc., docket 92-9341

**Historical event and date:** Amendment on denial of rehearing on 1994-12-23; 37 F.3d 881 (2d Cir. 1994). Only the selected event is described.

**Historical disposition:** Affirmed the interlocutory rejection of fair use for the stipulated copying, in the December 23, 1994 amended version.

**Historical panel:** Jon O. Newman, Ralph K. Winter, Jr., Dennis Jacobs.

**Opinion author and joins:** Jon O. Newman wrote the opinion. Joined by Winter.

**Historical holding and grounds:** The majority assessed the complete articles retained as reference copies under all four § 107 factors, giving weight to their substitute function and the evidence of an institutional copying-license market. The opinion did not decide the legality of every scientific photocopy or the remaining damages and injunction questions.

**Separate opinions:** Jacobs dissented, emphasizing the scientific purpose, the character of an individual researcher’s files, and the circularity risk of using an emerging permission market to defeat fair use.

**Remedy and mandate:** The limited fair-use determination was affirmed and the remaining district-court proceedings were left for completion. The selected event did not itself fix a comprehensive damages or licensing remedy. The separate mandate’s issuance date and any stay or recall status are not established by an independently supplied docket; they are left unknown.
