# American Geophysical Union v. Texaco, Inc.

## Historical disposition and comparison

Chief Judge Newman wrote the opinion, joined by Judge Winter, affirming the district court's rejection of fair use. Judge Jacobs dissented, emphasizing the scientific research purpose, the nature of an individual researcher's files, and the circularity risk in using emerging license fees to defeat fair use. The selected opinion was amended December 23, 1994. *American Geophysical Union*, 37 F.3d at 885-905.

Stone's proposed judgment matches the historical disposition but gives express independent weight to research at a for-profit institution and limits market reasoning to a demonstrated substitute-copying market. The further amendment reported at 60 F.3d 913 in July 1995 is identified for version control only; it supplies no later holding, rationale, or simulated vote in this brief.
