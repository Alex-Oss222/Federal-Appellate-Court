# Annual case-list template

Use this form for `years/YYYY/case-list.md`. Replace bracketed instructions with verified information. The [selection policy](../CASE_SELECTION.md) controls the six groups and eighteen-case cycles; the year is a filing location, not a cycle deadline.

## Annual header

- Period: calendar-year range, restricted to the actual opening date when applicable.
- Status: selecting / in progress / annually reconciled.
- Admitted cases first entering detailed play this year: count of canonical rows.
- Coverage: three cases in each of six groups per cycle, with unfinished cycles carried forward.
- Continuing work: link to Standing State; do not duplicate older canonical rows here.

## Canonical case entries

| Cycle | Case name | Circuit | Docket/citation | Historical stage and date | Primary group | Secondary subjects | Principal question | Source | Simulation status | Departure from history |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Entry rules

- Link the case name to its case folder. Use a natural case name and verified docket or citation, not a manufactured historical identifier.
- Cycle is the coverage-cycle number, such as 001. Primary group is its number and short label from the selection policy. Count one primary group only.
- Identify the historical entry stage and date. If the simulation later changes a procedural date, document that difference in the case record and Departure column without overwriting historical facts.
- Source includes a supporting primary-source link and enough identification to verify admission. Incomplete research candidates remain preparation notes until the required source check is satisfied.
- Status distinguishes admitted, prepared, blocked, played, or withdrawn. A played case can still have follow-up pending in Standing State. Link a played entry to its final record.
- Departure is `Not yet assessed` before play; afterward it gives the supported difference or absence of a difference and a final-record reference. Do not feed this column into neutral modeling.
- Keep rows in chronological entry order. Preserve one canonical row across calendar years, follow-up events, and cycle audits.
- Panel membership and substitution details are mandatory in the linked case packet even though this compact index does not repeat them.
- Zero admitted cases means an empty table with a clear zero count, not eighteen invented or placeholder case rows.
