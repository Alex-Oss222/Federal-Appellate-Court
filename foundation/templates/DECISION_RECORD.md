# Final appellate event record template

Create one file per actual completed simulation event under the case's `events/` folder. Replace instructions with supported content; omit inapplicable optional sections. Historical events admitted as compatible follow-up use the same compact identity and provenance fields but do not require simulated commitments or invented voting detail.

## Internal identity and provenance

- Case and docket; historical source citation, explicitly distinguished from the simulation record.
- Event type and exact date; argument/submission date where material.
- Event basis: simulated circuit action / verified compatible historical follow-up / verified institutional or legal change.
- Status: final, or clearly marked draft not yet authoritative.
- Legal cutoff, sources, and any material assumptions.
- Historical panel; simulation panel; replaced member and assignment basis.
- Stage input references, frozen commitment references, and actual context-isolation record for a simulated adjudication.
- Stone approval reference where applicable.
- Correction lineage, only if this corrects an earlier entry with authorization.

## Public decision

### Case and court

Include the public caption, docket, court, relevant argument or submission date, event/decision date, panel or other disclosed participation, and publication status. This metadata must be available here, not solely in the internal provenance block, so the renderer can obey its reading boundary. Distinguish the historical source citation from the simulation opinion.

### Posture and issues

Identify the decision under review, the material record, jurisdiction, preserved issues, and the applicable review standards. Include the legally relevant dates.

### Judgment

State the disposition and relief by separable component. Name the supporting and opposing judges only to the extent the public action supports disclosure. For ordinary panel merits, reconcile all participating judges with the stated result. Do not invent an undisclosed rehearing poll or Supreme Court tally.

### Opinions and holdings

Identify each writing, author, and exact joins. For every controlling proposition state the operative rule, trigger, qualifications, supporting judges, legal authority, and application to the record. Explain the principal contrary argument and what is left open. Distinguish agreement in judgment from agreement in reasoning. Include substantive summaries of material concurrences and dissents without converting them to holdings.

For each material test applied or changed, give the actual formulation used, conditions, burdens, and review standard, with authority. These details stay in this final record; they do not require separate Holdings or Standards and Tests registers.

### Precedent and legal effect

State publication or nonprecedential status under the applicable rules, what prior decisions are followed or otherwise treated, and what law changes. Identify circuit-wide precedent separately from party-specific relief, law of the case, preclusion, dicta, and separate positions.

### Remedy and next stage

Specify the remedy, scope of remand, mandate status, and any verified next procedural step. Do not infer that an opinion date is the mandate date or that a historical Supreme Court outcome automatically follows.

### Public source notes, if supplied

Include only public-source references intended for publication. Private approvals, execution details, and historical-comparator analysis remain internal. Use the [render template](RENDER.md) to check that the public decision supplies every material item the chosen form needs.

## Internal follow-up and state changes

- Link the current Standing State row or state that no further proceeding is known. Record the origin year of the first selected circuit decision and retain it through review and remand.
- Link verified Supreme Court captions and docket numbers to the original circuit case. Identify each follow-up event by case, docket, date, type, and source so a later turn does not apply it twice.
- State the follow-up check cutoff and its source limitations. If petition status is unknown, create or retain a research task; do not infer that a petition is pending or that review is over.
- State any actual deadline and its source, or a research task if status is unknown.
- For historical follow-up, document whether the changed judgment, review-seeking party, questions, posture, relief, and timing support retaining the event.
- Record incompatible historical events separately and describe the unresolved live path. No substitute Supreme Court result may be generated.
- Name indexed holdings affected by this event, their effective dates, and any superseding historical Supreme Court law.
- Give a short validation result with concrete unresolved gaps; do not include private model transcripts.
