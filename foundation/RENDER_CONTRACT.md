# Appellate decision presentation

Render only the final record's Public decision section. Read this contract, the [render template](templates/RENDER.md), and the authorized final public material, not Stone's private input, historical comparison, frozen commitments, or later case history. Presentation cannot determine or revise legal substance. Use the template's merits form or procedural-event form as appropriate and remove unused instructions and placeholders.

Use the form the decision needs, ordinarily:

1. Caption, docket, court, panel, submission/argument date, and decision date.
2. Posture, material facts, and issues properly before the court.
3. Disposition and named panel alignment by component.
4. Holding, governing standard, authority, and application to the record.
5. Material separate opinions and their actual joins.
6. Limits, treatment of precedent, publication status, and resulting legal effect.
7. Remedy, mandate status, and next procedural step when known.

A routine case can be brief. Use a table only when mixed dispositions or partial joins require it. Do not force a full set of merits sections onto a petition entry or procedural order. Do not invent a public voting tally for an action whose sources disclose none.

Write clear judicial prose. Explain what each authority supplies. Distinguish the court's rule from a concurrence, dissent, or proposed future rule. Preserve operative exceptions and conditions; preserve the difference between permission and obligation. Do not add a finding, quote, rationale, or legal effect missing from the record.

Keep context identifiers, user approvals, prompts, workflow notes, correction provenance, and model details out of the public decision. The repository-level framing identifies the work as a simulation. Internal history remains in the event record and Git.

If the final record lacks a material item, identify the gap for the operator and return the affected item for correction. Do not use rendering to readjudicate it. A correction updates the affected public entry from the corrected final record while leaving unrelated entries unchanged.
