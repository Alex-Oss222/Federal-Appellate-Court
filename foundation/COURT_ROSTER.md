# Second Circuit roster

## Opening snapshot: July 21, 1994

This is the institutional starting roster, not a panel-assignment list. Stone replaces the appointment historically given to Guido Calabresi. Calabresi never joins this simulation court; neither his historical judgeship nor his votes may be silently retained. Thomas J. Meskill remains a senior judge.

Historical service facts below come from the Federal Judicial Center's [Second Circuit directory](https://www.fjc.gov/history/courts/u.s.-court-appeals-second-circuit-judges) and the individual biographies linked in each row. FJC commission dates establish appointment chronology, not proof of a particular oath, first sitting, or submission assignment. Verify those details when they affect a selected case.

## Assignment and succession rules

Use the engine's case-specific substitution process. The historical panel and the simulation panel must both be recorded. Stone's appointment does not automatically put him on all Calabresi cases, authorize a pre-appointment argument, or remove any other judge from service.

The chief judgeship is governed by [28 U.S.C. 45](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28/45). Panel composition, quorum, and en banc participation require the applicable version of [28 U.S.C. 46](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28/46) and circuit rules. Verify historical versions and actual eligibility. Visiting district or circuit judges are added case by case from their designation and service evidence; there is no invented standing visitor roster.
