# Appellate simulation instructions

This repository runs selected historical Second Circuit appeals beginning January 1, 1994, Stone's first full calendar year after his fictional commission and required judicial oaths on December 1, 1993. The user controls Judge Alex-Lamar Stone-Khoury. Follow [the engine](foundation/ENGINE.md). This is a separate project from `scotus-career`; its old instructions, permissions, case outcomes, and standing fallbacks do not apply here.

## Authority and reading boundaries

- The engine controls simulation procedure and source roles. Governing historical law still comes from verified legal sources, not from a character document.
- [Case selection](foundation/CASE_SELECTION.md) controls six coverage groups, three historical cases per group, and eighteen-case cycles across calendar years. Use the [case-list template](foundation/templates/CASE_LIST.md); keep one canonical row per case. Audit each completed cycle and carry unfinished group slots in Standing State.
- [Standing State](state/STANDING_STATE.md) identifies the current date and pending work. Maintain it with the [Standing State template](foundation/templates/STANDING_STATE.md), preserving existing live entries. [The roster](foundation/COURT_ROSTER.md) supplies dated institutional facts. Future roster entries are scheduling information only.
- [Precedent](state/PRECEDENT.md) is an index. Final event records and effective historical authorities control legal substance. Do not create separate Holdings or Standards and Tests registers; preserve holdings and applied tests in each final record.
- [Character](stone/CHARACTER.md) controls biography only. It supplies neither a court holding, a colleague's agreement, nor Stone's case-specific position.
- `stone/JURISPRUDENCE.md` is archival storage only, never an engine input. Do not open, read, search, index, summarize, embed, import, validate, or otherwise process its contents. This exclusion applies to preparation, research, generation, neutral modeling, reconciliation, assembly, validation, follow-up, and rendering. Do not import engine-generated summaries, embeddings, digests, cached extracts, or other archival derivatives, including previously created ones. The user's expressly supplied case-specific brief is an authorized input; do not disqualify it based on hypothetical provenance. The archive exclusion never authorizes comparing that brief with the archive or reconstructing the archive.
- The latest user-approved case brief `stone.md` is the sole source of Stone's case-specific disposition and reasoning, within the law and its stated scope. Record choices covered by a genuine existing delegation in that brief without seeking duplicate approval. Ask for the user's case-specific input only when neither the approved brief nor a genuine existing delegation covers a required choice; never use the archive, biography, or an old project permission as a fallback. A conflict with binding authority must be identified and resolved before assembly.
- [Render rules](foundation/RENDER_CONTRACT.md) and the [render template](foundation/templates/RENDER.md) govern presentation only. All reusable forms are linked in the [template index](foundation/templates/README.md).

Read only the sources needed for the stage, using explicit allowed-file lists for reads, searches, retrieval, and validation. Exclude the jurisprudence archive and engine-generated archival derivatives from every list, including maintenance workflows. Do not preload Stone's case brief or biography, the historical case result, or future legal developments into a neutral modeling context.

## Run stages

Use [the brief template](foundation/CASE_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md) and these separate files under `years/YYYY/cases/<case-slug>/`:

1. Preparation creates `neutral.md`, `stone.md`, and `history.md`. A preparer who has read all three cannot act as the isolated neutral modeler in the same context.
2. A **fresh neutral context** sees only the neutral packet, its date-eligible authorities, relevant public prior decisions, and procedural engine rules. It models the participating judges other than Stone and saves `commitments.md`.
3. A **separate reconciliation context** sees the neutral material, frozen commitments, and historical comparison, but never Stone's private material. It saves `reconciled.md`.
4. Assembly receives the frozen reconciled positions and the approved Stone proposal. It determines actual agreement on judgment, reasoning, remedy, authorship, and separate writings. Save a dated final event record.
5. Automatically check review follow-up for every unresolved origin-year case before each authorized calendar advance, process due events in chronological order, and check again before rendering. The three-year view includes older unresolved carryovers and never fixes Supreme Court event dates. Update Standing State and the precedent index from completed final events. End every simulation turn with the template's eight-section Form A court report, displayed directly to the user and saved, without waiting for a separate Render request. Explain the current case, any completed outcome, the reasoning, and supported practical context; include written opinions for decisions completed in that turn. Keep unchanged sections brief and report an undecided case as undecided. Limit case entries to selected cases and their connected review proceedings, including earlier-year carryovers; other decisions appear only as necessary authority. Supreme Court updates must explain their effect on the finalized simulated appellate judgment. The report uses the contract's separately prepared, dated administrative and public-source extracts; never feed that combined report to neutral modeling. Individual decision renders still receive only the final record's public decision, render contract, and template and cannot add legal substance. Repository maintenance or standalone explanatory questions do not themselves require a simulation report.

Delegate the substantive neutral and reconciliation stages to separate scoped agents, fresh tasks, or equivalent fresh executions when running a case. Pass an explicit allowed-file list and no inherited conversation containing Stone or the comparator. A new filename, prompt section, or instruction to forget is not isolation. In Codex cloud each isolated stage is its own task, as [the runbook](foundation/RUN.md) describes. If fresh contexts are unavailable, finish preparation and report that specific limitation; do not claim an isolated Run occurred. These requirements do not call for agents when merely editing infrastructure.

## Chat commands

"Run this year" (`/run-year`) and "run this session" (`/run-session`) start the orchestrated run in [the runbook](foundation/RUN.md). The year is the simulation year in Standing State, not the real date. The command authorizes the run, not Stone's positions: `stone.md` is the user's case-specific brief, so stop that case until it exists and is user-approved, except for choices the user's own words delegate. Resume from the file ledger with `python3 tools/court.py plan`, which prints the task sentence for each case's next stage: `Prepare <slug>`, `Neutral stage: <slug>`, `Reconcile stage: <slug>`, `Assemble stage: <slug>`, `Render stage: <slug>`, `Compare stage: <slug>`, and `Finalize: <slug>`. Each is a separate task that follows [the runbook](foundation/RUN.md); in Codex cloud a stage task is its own fresh context. The script checks bookkeeping, stages isolated inputs, files a finished stage's single output, and draws the replaced panelist and the author; it never chooses legal meaning or votes.

## Working rules

- Supreme Court membership and decisions remain historical. Never generate a replacement Supreme Court vote, holding, grant, denial, or remand.
- Select real historical Second Circuit appeals under the case-selection policy. Preserve their historical parties, dispute, material facts, claims, and pre-simulation procedure. A case-specific panel substitution is a declared simulation premise, not a discovered historical fact. Case-list departures and future outcomes are not neutral inputs.
- Keep the two supplied Stone documents substantively unchanged unless the user requests edits. Never revise an approved Stone proposal silently.
- Validate chronology, panel eligibility, vote and join arithmetic, authority status, record links, and follow-up continuity. Scripts may check bookkeeping but may not choose legal meaning or judicial votes.
- Use primary sources for legal propositions and the relevant date's statutes and rules. Do not transplant modern appellate rules into 1994.
- Record only concise conclusions, grounds, sources, and material uncertainties. Do not generate hidden reasoning transcripts or fictional conference dialogue.
- Stop only the affected case or issue for a material gap. Continue independent authorized work.
- User-authorized corrections replace the affected final record in place, identify the correction internally, and update dependent indexes and renders. Preserve history in Git. Do not silently change other completed cases.
- Update effective law and pending status after each final event. Annual close is reconciliation, not a new adjudication or a requirement to duplicate every historical holding.
